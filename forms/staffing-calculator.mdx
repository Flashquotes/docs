## Overview

The Staffing Calculator question on a lead intake form collects the guest count and service length, then shows customers how many staff members their event needs. Staff numbers themselves are resolved from per-service [Staff & Resource Rules](/services/staff-resource-rules) — the form editor no longer defines any staffing math.

<Info>
Configure staff and resource counts under **Services & items → Staffing Rules** for each service. The form editor only controls what the customer sees; the numbers come from your service rules.
</Info>

## Where staff counts come from

When a customer submits your form, Flashquotes resolves staff and resource counts from the [Staff & Resource Rules](/services/staff-resource-rules) of the selected services. If a service has no rule, that service is treated as unresolvable and the customer sees a deferred message instead of a number. See [How rules resolve on the lead form](#how-rules-resolve-on-the-lead-form) below.

To make the resolution source visible in the editor, the Staffing Calculator question's settings panel shows a banner naming which rule(s) will drive the number:

- **Using staffing rules for selected services: [service names]** — the preview has services selected and each of them has a rule.
- **Using staffing rules from your default service, [service name]. Change default service** — no services are selected in the preview, so the form's default service supplies the rule.
- **Set a default service to calculate staff from your staffing rules.** — no default service is set. Choose one on the form's settings page.
- **Add a service to calculate staff from your staffing rules.** — the company has no services yet. Add one under **Services & items** first.

The banner reflects your standing configuration, not just the current preview: a transient preview selection can refine the message but never masks a misconfiguration.

## Question settings

### Guest count

- **Starts at** — the guest count the counter opens with.
- **Adjust by** — how much the +/- buttons change the count each click.

Both values are clamped to sensible ranges.

### Service length

- **Minimum hours** — the shortest service duration you offer.
- **Maximum hours** — the longest service duration available.

Supports half-hour increments.

### Staff visual counter

The visual counter shows an interactive row of staff avatars on the lead form that updates as the customer changes the guest count or service length.

#### Turn the visual counter on or off

Use the **Enable visual counter** toggle:

- **On** — the form displays the counter with an avatar row that scales with the resolved staff count. Customization fields appear below the toggle.
- **Off** — the form shows a text-only question instead. Customize the question title and subtitle; staffing still resolves behind the scenes and lands on the quote.

#### Customize staff titles and photos

With the visual counter enabled, personalize:

- **Staff title (singular / plural)** — for example, "barista" / "baristas".
- **Staff description** — for example, "Serving lattes, cappuccinos, and more".
- **Staff images** — click any of the five image slots to upload or select a photo of your team. Images are optional; hover a photo and click the X to remove it.

<Tip>
Want to replace the default staff icon with a photo of your own team? Enable the visual counter, then click an image slot under **Staff images** to upload your photo.
</Tip>

## How rules resolve on the lead form

The visual counter names what it's showing and, when it can't show a number, gets out of the way:

- **Attribution line** — under the count, the form shows **Estimated for [service]**, naming the service(s) the number is based on. Up to three services are named ("A", "A and B", or "A, B and C"); four or more collapse to "A, B and 2 more".
- **Deferred state** — when no rule resolves (no selected service has a rule, no default service supplies one, or the company has no services), the counter is hidden entirely. In its place, the form shows a single line using your own staff noun, such as *"We'll confirm how many professional baristas you need when we send your quote."* The guest count and service length inputs stay — those are still worth collecting.
- **Face pile** — the avatar row is capped at five circles. Beyond that, four avatars appear alongside a **+N** chip (capped at **+99**) so the row never changes width. The exact count leads the block regardless.
