---
title: "Find anything with search"
description: "Jump to any page, record, or action in Flashquotes without touching your mouse."
---

Search gets you anywhere in Flashquotes in a few keystrokes — no need to click through menus.

## Open search

Press **⌘K** (Mac) or **Ctrl+K** (Windows) from any page. Or click the **Search** button at the top of the sidebar, which shows the shortcut.

## Browse before you type

Open search and you'll see three sections right away:

- **On this page** — shortcuts for what you're viewing. On a quote, that's actions like Send quote, Preview as customer, Copy quote link, and Download PDF.
- **Recent** — records you've looked at recently.
- **Common actions** — your most-used pages and actions. This list adapts as you use Flashquotes.

## Search by keyword

Start typing to search:

- **Every page**, including Settings pages. Keywords match broadly — typing "tax" surfaces both the Sales tax report and Tax rates settings.
- **Your records** — contacts, quotes, invoices, bookings, and events. Contacts match by name, email, company, or phone number.
- **Actions** like New contact and New quote.

## Navigate results

Use the arrow keys to move through results, **Enter** to open the highlighted one, and **Esc** to close search.
