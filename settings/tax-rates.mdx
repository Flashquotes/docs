Manage the sales tax rates flashquotes applies to quotes and invoices.

Go to **Settings > Tax Rates**.

## Add a tax rate

1. Click **Add tax rate**.
2. Enter a name (e.g., "CO Sales Tax") and the rate. Rates support up to 4 decimal places.
3. Click **Add tax rate** to save.

## Change a rate

Rates can't be edited after creation. To change one:

1. Add a new tax rate with the updated percentage.
2. Delete the old rate.
3. Update your location's default tax rate in **Settings > Company > Location**.

## Set the default rate

Each location has one default tax rate, set in [Company settings](/settings/general#location-management). New quotes, including auto-drafted quotes, prepopulate with this rate.

Need a different rate for a specific quote, like a county-specific rate? Open the quote and pick a different rate from the tax rate dropdown. You can also mark individual line items as taxable or not.

## How changes affect quotes

- Changing the default or deleting a rate never changes existing quotes. They keep the rate they were created with.
- New quotes pick up the current default.

**What clients see:** Tax appears as its own line in the quote totals. It can't be hidden.

Common issues:
- Quotes still show an old rate: existing quotes keep their original rate. Update each quote's tax rate manually.
- Auto-quotes apply the wrong county rate: auto-quotes always use the location default. Adjust the rate on the quote after it's created.
